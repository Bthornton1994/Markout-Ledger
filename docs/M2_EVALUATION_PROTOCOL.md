# Milestone 2: recorded-market evaluation is not fill calibration

This document fixes what a replay of recorded market observations can and cannot establish, how fill assumptions are reported, and when a result must be called inconclusive. It applies to every run on a `synthetic: false` fixture produced under [M2_DATA_CONTRACT.md](M2_DATA_CONTRACT.md) (venue and instrument: Kraken spot `BTC/USD`, [M2_DATA_SOURCE_DECISION.md](M2_DATA_SOURCE_DECISION.md)). Every output of this protocol computed from a recorded capture (grids, held-out results, reference statistics, tables, reports and figures) is published in this repository or its pull requests only as far as the owner's attested clearance of decision condition C2's use (iv) covers it (M2_DATA_SOURCE_DECISION.md §1); until then it stays with the owner. Nothing in this document is a permission, and C2 and C3 are unmet.

## 1. Two separate questions

| question | inputs | what answers it |
|---|---|---|
| **Recorded-market evaluation.** On real order flow, does steering change the paper outcome relative to the unsteered policy and the no-trade baseline, under a stated fill model? | recorded observations with genuine receive timestamps; the paper execution model, whose textual assumptions are in `replay_started.assumptions` and whose numeric ones are in `replay_started.config` | replaying `no_trade`, `unsteered`, `steered` on identical input, reported alongside the fill-uncertainty counters |
| **Fill calibration.** How do real resting orders on this venue actually get acknowledged, queued, filled and cancelled? | our own tiny-size orders with venue acknowledgements, queue position where reported, fill and cancel outcomes, measured latencies | a measured fill model, which does not exist yet |

Paper P&L is a function of assumed fills. Without own-order acknowledgements and fills, **no paper P&L figure in this repository establishes profitability**, and no report may say otherwise. The M1 claim, "steering changes behaviour", is testable on recorded data; "steering is profitable" is not, until calibration data exists (DATA_REQUIREMENTS.md, item 5). Milestone 2 does not place orders and produces no calibration data.

## 2. The fill-assumption sensitivity report

Every recorded-session result is reported as a grid, never as a single number. The grid re-runs the same three run kinds on the same fixture under each assumption set; the fixture content hash, the ledger head hashes and the assumption set (read from that run's `replay_started.config`, or carried by the grid tool from its own inputs) are printed with every row. The smallest M2 PR produces no grid: its real-session table (handoff A12) is one cell under the recorded scenario's defaults and is labelled a mechanics check, inconclusive by construction (I1 and I4 both hold: fewer than 4 cells, and fewer than 3 held-out sessions). The grid is produced with `replay()` in a later PR (REPLAY.md, "Programmatic use").

Dimensions, all `ReplayConfig` / `ExecutionConfig` fields (REPLAY.md "Configuration limits"):

| dimension | values | why |
|---|---|---|
| `execution.orderLatencyMs` / `execution.cancelLatencyMs` | 0, 50, 150, 500 ms (paired) | activation and cancel instants decide venue-time eligibility; fixed constants stand in for a distribution we have not measured |
| `maxStalenessMs` | 200, 500, 1000 ms (outcome horizons kept `>=` it) | decides which prints are discarded as stale and how wide the reordering window is |
| `execution.makerFeeBps` | 0 and the maker rate recorded under condition C4 of M2_DATA_SOURCE_DECISION.md for the capture day: the rate that applies at the assumed tier (Tier 1 unless the owner documents another) to the instrument selected after the P0 listing, re-read that day for that instrument on the fee schedule, the maker-rebate eligible-pairs list (searched under every name of the instrument selected after the P0 listing: `BTC/USD`, `XBT/USD`, `XBTUSD` and `XXBTZUSD` for `BTC/USD` (its WebSocket symbol and REST `wsname`, `altname` and key), the WebSocket and REST names for a substitute pair), the stablecoin and FX fee page and, whenever a tier other than Tier 1 is assumed, the cross-platform tier-change article, entered as the smallest non-negative whole number of basis points not below it (a rebate is entered as 0), with the rate as read and the entered value both recorded | the tier of an account that does not exist is an assumption, the schedule can change before the capture day, and the eligible-pairs list can change (neither `BTC/USD` nor `XBT/USD` was on it at the owner's reading reported 2026-09-23 [O]) |
| `execution.placementCost` / `execution.cancelCost` | 0 and the venue's per-message cost if any (no per-message order-entry cost was found for this venue, which is not verified [U]: the owner checks the fee page, M2_DATA_SOURCE_DECISION.md §4; gas on an on-chain venue) | messaging costs dominate a requoting policy |

The fee dimension can dominate. At the Tier 1 maker rate the owner reported on 2026-09-23 [O] (0.40%, that is 40 bps; M2_DATA_SOURCE_DECISION.md C4; a dated reading, not a guarantee for any capture day), a filled quote in the recorded scenario pays 40 bps of notional against a quoted half spread of 2 bps (8 bps at the controller's widest multiplier; contract §8.6). The fee-bearing cells therefore measure mostly the fee; the 0 bps cells show the execution mechanics. At that rate fees alone reach the scenario's 20 USD `maxLoss` after roughly 80 fills of 0.001 BTC near 63 000 USD, fewer at larger size multipliers or prices, so a fee-bearing cell, including the A12 mechanics check, may end at the kill switch and be dropped under I7; the A12 report states whether and when it tripped. Neither kind of cell says anything about profitability (§1).

The queue model is not a field: every run reports the constant `pessimistic_back_of_queue` (`fillUncertainty.model` carries the token; `replay_started.config.execution.queueModel` carries the full sentence that begins with it). An optimistic bound (`optimistic_front_of_queue`) is a follow-up change to `src/execution/paper.ts`, not part of the smallest M2 PR; until it exists the report states that only the pessimistic bound was run.

Columns per row (all in `RunSummary`, i.e. `results.json` `runs.<kind>`): `portfolio.netPnl`, `portfolio.grossRealized`, `portfolio.feesPaid`, `portfolio.txCostsPaid`, `fills.count`, `fills.qty`, `fills.uncertain`, `fillUncertainty.queueConsumedWithoutFill`, `fills.lateAfterCancel`, `fills.ineligible`, `outcomes[*].avgMarkoutBps` and `count`, `orders.rejectedByRisk`, `risk.killSwitchTripped`, `observations.rejected`, `outcomesPendingAtEnd`; plus the per-run `config` block and `quotesOutsideVisibleBook` that the M2 build adds to `results.json` (contract §7; the grid tool calls the same shared library function on each run's ledger and fixture, so the column exists per cell).

Derived per session and assumption set: `steered - unsteered` and `steered - no_trade` net P&L, and the same differences for the shortest-horizon markout.

Fill uncertainty is part of the result, not a footnote: a row whose `fills.uncertain` or `queueConsumedWithoutFill` is large is a row the data could not decide.

## 3. Held-out session protocol

1. **Session unit.** One session = one segment (contract §5.8) of at least 60 minutes whose `endReason` is `capture_end`, `checksum_mismatch`, `snapshot_reset`, `gap_disconnect` or `clock_cut` (the emitted range of every segment is verified up to its cut; segments ended by `reconstruction_failure` or `malformed_depth` are excluded as a conservative rule because those reasons mean the adapter met a state it could not explain, a crossed book after verified checksums or a malformed frame, rather than a feed event), from a capture host with a recorded clock-sync report, normalized to one fixture with one content hash.
2. **Development set.** Sessions used while writing or tuning policy parameters, controller rules or fill assumptions. Anything inspected before the rule set is frozen is development data, whatever it was intended to be.
3. **Freeze.** Before any held-out session is *captured*, commit to the repository: the exact policy and controller versions (commit hash), the assumption grid, the metrics, and the decision rule below. The commit hash is the pre-registration timestamp. A statistic computed from a capture (such as the reference set of step 4) is a derived output, and so is any hash of it. The freeze commit carries its value only within the attested scope of C2 use (iv). Otherwise the owner keeps the value and a secret random nonce, and the freeze commit carries the SHA-256 of the value's canonical JSON followed by that nonce only if the attested clearance covers publishing that commitment; if neither is covered, the owner keeps the value, the nonce and the commitment with a dated record outside the repository, and the freeze commit states only that the reference set was fixed and is held by the owner. In that last case the reference set's pre-registration rests on the owner's dated record, not on the commit hash, and every result that uses the set says so.
4. **Held-out set.** At least 3 sessions captured after the freeze, on at least 3 distinct calendar days, including at least one session whose realized volatility, defined as the standard deviation of `windowRows[*].midChangeBps` over the session's windows in the `no_trade` run, is above the third quartile or below the first quartile of the same statistic over the development sessions as they stood at the freeze (a fixed reference set, fixed at the freeze and carried by the freeze commit in one of the forms step 3 allows). Held-out fixtures are replayed once per grid cell and never used to change anything; if a change is made afterwards, the sessions become development data and new held-out sessions are needed.
5. **Statistic.** Per-window net P&L is the increment `windowRows[k].netPnl - windowRows[k-1].netPnl` (with `windowRows[0].netPnl` for `k = 0`; `WindowRow.netPnl` itself is the cumulative valuation at the window end). `windowRows[k].markoutShortest` is already per window. For each held-out session and grid cell, form the paired per-window difference `steered - unsteered` of each metric. Increments are serially dependent (positions and resting orders carry across window boundaries), so the interval is a **block bootstrap** over windows: non-overlapping blocks of length `L = max(2, ceil(N ^ (1/3)))` for `N` windows, `2000` resamples drawn with a fixed seed (`20260922`), and the percentile interval (the 5th and 95th percentiles of the resampled means). `L` is reported per session. Report the mean paired difference per session and cell, its 90% interval, and the sign agreement across sessions and cells.

## 4. When the result is inconclusive (explicit criteria)

Scope of each criterion: I2, I3, I6, I7 and I8 are **cell-scoped**, evaluated per session and grid cell on that cell's runs; a failing cell is dropped from the grid for that session and reported as dropped. I1, I4, I5 and I9 are **evaluation-scoped**, computed over the remaining cells only. A recorded-market evaluation is **inconclusive**, and must be reported as such, if any evaluation-scoped criterion holds or if fewer than 4 cells remain for any session:

| code | criterion |
|---|---|
| I1 | The sign of the mean `steered - unsteered` net P&L difference is not the same across all remaining cells of the assumption grid for the same session (the conclusion depends on an unmeasured assumption), or fewer than 4 cells remain. |
| I2 | In either trading run of the cell, `fills.uncertain` (which counts order-print pairs) exceeds 5% of `fills.count`, or the summed size of stale-discarded eligible prints (the distinct `tradeEventId` values of `fill_uncertain` ledger entries, each joined once to the fixture's trade event; one discarded print yields one entry per eligible order, so entries are deduplicated by print; the rejected observation carries no size in the ledger) exceeds 5% of `fills.qty`. |
| I3 | `fillUncertainty.queueConsumedWithoutFill` exceeds `fills.count` in the steered or unsteered run of the cell (most touches were decided by the queue guess, not by prints). |
| I4 | Fewer than 30 fills in the steered or unsteered run of any remaining cell of a session, or fewer than 3 held-out sessions, or held-out sessions on fewer than 3 distinct days. |
| I5 | On the net P&L increment (the markout difference is reported, not tested): a session is undecided when its 90% block-bootstrap interval of the mean paired difference contains zero in more than half of its remaining cells; I5 holds when a majority of held-out sessions are undecided. |
| I6 | Capture quality (per cell): the trade-stream `lagStatsMs.p99` exceeds the cell's `maxStalenessMs`; `observations.rejected` exceeds 1% of `observations.accepted`; `clockSync` is `unknown`; with millisecond probes (`venueClockOffsetMs.resolutionMs == 1`) `medianMs` exceeds 25 ms in absolute value; with second-resolution probes only (`resolutionMs == 1000`, contract §5.6) the cell fails, because no bound comparable to 25 ms can be established at that resolution (a documentation excerpt shows venue times of millisecond or finer resolution on subscription acknowledgements [S], so a segment normally has at least the three samples the millisecond branch needs); `integrity.checksumMismatches` exceeds 1, or equals 1 while `endReason` is not `checksum_mismatch` (impossible by construction, kept as an assertion). |
| I7 | The kill switch tripped in either trading run of the cell (the comparison is then between a stopped run and a running one). |
| I9 | The per-session mean paired net P&L differences (each session's mean over its remaining cells) do not all share one sign across the held-out sessions. |
| I8 | Visible book coverage (per cell): in the steered or unsteered run, `quotesOutsideVisibleBook.count` exceeds 1% of `quotesOutsideVisibleBook.ofLive` (orders rested beyond the deepest level the fixture shows on their side are seeded with a queue of zero, which is the optimistic direction; contract §5.3, EXECUTION_MODEL.md). The fixture's `visibleSpanBps` is printed with every row so the depth can be raised for the next capture rather than the policy narrowed after the fact. |

A conclusive result is stated per session, never pooled: "on N held-out sessions of venue/instrument, under assumption grid G with the cells dropped by I2, I3, I6, I7 and I8 listed, the volatility reference set published, committed by salted hash, or held by the owner without a public commitment at the freeze, steering changed net paper P&L per window by X_s (90% block-bootstrap interval) in session s, with the same sign in every session and every remaining cell; the pessimistic queue model was the only fill model run; this is not evidence of profitability". Nothing stronger is admissible until fill calibration data exists.

## 5. What Milestone 2 changes and what it does not

- Adds: recorded observations with genuine receive timestamps, the sensitivity grid, the held-out protocol.
- Does not add: own-order data, a calibrated queue model, market impact, or any statement about live profitability. The execution model's stated limitations (EXECUTION_MODEL.md, "Remaining uncertainty" and "Not modeled") all still apply.
