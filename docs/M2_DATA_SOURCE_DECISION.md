## 1. Decision

**Selected provisionally: Kraken spot `BTC/USD`, captured first party from the public WebSocket API v2 (`wss://ws.kraken.com/v2`): the `book` channel at depth 100 with its in-band snapshot and per-message CRC32 checksum, the `trade` channel, and the `instrument` channel.** Adapter mapping: M2_DATA_CONTRACT.md §8. The selection holds only while conditions C1 to C5 below can be met; the P0 listing does not run until C2 and C3 are met, and nothing is captured until C2, C3 and C5 are met.

Why this one:

- The venue serves clients in the United States [S], where this project is operated; the official US quick-start page (`https://support.kraken.com/articles/quick-start-for-clients-in-the-united-states`) names New York and Maine as the states whose residents are not served, per search excerpts of 2026-09-22 and 2026-09-23 [S] (C3). Bybit, the previous selection, excludes the United States on its official restricted-countries page (`bybit.com/en/help-center/article/Service-Restricted-Countries`) [S], so its capture requirement was not executable; Binance's Terms of Use name the United States among its Restricted Locations and access from the US is reported blocked (HTTP 451) [S `binance.com/en/terms`].
- Public market data needs no account or key: the official Go SDK subscribes to `book`, `trade` and `instrument` with no token and calls `/0/public/*` without authentication [R]; the venue's WebSocket FAQ and introduction say the same [S].
- The book can be reconstructed **fail closed**: every book message carries a CRC32 checksum of the top 10 levels per side after the message is applied; the algorithm is implemented in the venue's official Go SDK and pinned with the venue's documented worked example in its official Rust SDK tests [R]. No sequence number exists on book messages (none in any official type or fixture [R]; none located in documentation [U]), and none is assumed.
- Trades name the taker side (`side`), carry a per-book sequential `trade_id`, the taker order type and an RFC 3339 timestamp with microseconds [R types, S descriptions]; several trades may share one message and batching does not imply one taker order [S].
- The instrument channel publishes explicit `price_increment`, `qty_increment`, `price_precision` and `qty_precision` per pair [R types, S example], so the precision check of contract §5.5 is mechanical.
- Every message shape, the checksum, the reconnection behaviour and the keepalive cadence were read from the venue's official repositories at pinned commits [R] (§5), so the adapter can be implemented without access to the website; the remaining semantics are [S] and listed as owner reading (§4).

Conditions attached to the decision (each is blocking until met; none is a checklist nicety):

The normalizer refuses the instrument on the current engine (contract §5.5, R5). Either the versioned precision migration lands first (M2_GROK_HANDOFF.md §0, PR-0) or the owner substitutes a Kraken pair with `qty_precision <= 6` and `price_precision <= 6` after the P0 listing; no such pair has been verified from here (§4). The choice between PR-0 and a substitute is not made before the P0 listing exists, and the listing itself waits for C2 and C3 (handoff §0). Nothing is rounded or truncated in any case.

### 3a. Gemini, Bitstamp and Binance.US

The three US-accessible alternatives were evaluated on the same checklist by the research pass of 2026-09-22 (rows above). Gemini and Binance.US publish official documentation or SDKs on GitHub, so their feed mechanics are [R]; both synchronize a book with `U`/`u` update ids against a snapshot and publish no checksum, both quote BTC quantities at `1e-8` (the same migration need as Kraken), and neither's US state availability, terms or fee schedule could be read (their availability, terms and fee pages could not be read from the authoring environment). Bitstamp has no official GitHub organisation; everything known about it is third party, and what is known (no in-band snapshot, no sequence number or checksum, crossed books documented as expected) rejects it on data mechanics. None was selected. If C2 cannot be met, Gemini and Binance.US are the two candidates to evaluate next, on a Binance-style adapter with the buffered-snapshot fold rule that this contract deliberately does not carry (contract §5.3, last bullet).
